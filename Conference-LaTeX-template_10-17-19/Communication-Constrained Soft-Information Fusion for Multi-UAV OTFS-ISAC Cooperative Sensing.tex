	\begin{abstract}
		In multi-UAV OTFS-ISAC networks, inter-UAV soft-information exchange can
		improve cooperative sensing, but excessive signaling overhead, exchange
		delay, and unreliable reporting links may offset the sensing gain. To address
		this issue, this paper proposes a communication-error-calibrated and
		coarse-to-fine (C2F) delay-Doppler (DD)-aware Lagrangian link-selection
		method for communication-constrained multi-UAV cooperative sensing. The
		proposed method evaluates each target-dependent cooperative link according to
		its calibrated marginal deflection gain, reporting reliability, exchange
		delay, and DD-domain energy-capture efficiency. A coarse DD-domain Lagrangian utility
		is first used to screen candidate links, and local DD-grid refinement is then
		applied only to shortlisted candidates, thereby reducing unnecessary fine-grid
		evaluations. Simulation results in a 15-UAV, 10-target aerial sensing
		scenario show that the proposed method retains about 94.8\% of the
		all-neighbor detection probability while reducing signaling overhead and
		exchange delay by approximately 92.1\% and 94.8\%, respectively. Under the
		same per-target selected-link budget, it outperforms Sensing-SINR-based
		selection and achieves comparable detection to Top-K Deflection with about
		29\% lower exchange delay.
	\end{abstract}
