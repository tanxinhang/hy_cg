\section{Waveform-Calibrated Sensing Extension}
\label{sec:waveform_calibrated_extension}

The released V1 uses an analytical delay--Doppler (DD) proxy to rank sensing
triplets.  We retain that model as the reproducible reference and introduce a
separate waveform-calibrated extension.  The extension maps an OTFS point-spread
function (PSF) to the local detector distribution; it does not yet model a
standards-compliant pulse-shaped OTFS receiver.

\subsection{Waveform-Equivalent Local Observation}

For sensing triplet $e=(i,j,q)$ and incoherent look
$r\in\{1,\ldots,K_{\rm look}\}$, let $\mathcal W_e$ denote the local DD
window and write its vectorized matched-filter output as
\begin{equation}
 \mathbf y_e^{(r)}=
 \alpha_{e,q}^{(r)}\mathbf h_{e,q}
 +\sum_{q'\ne q}\alpha_{e,q'}^{(r)}\mathbf h_{e,q'}
 +\mathbf c_e^{(r)}+\mathbf m_e^{(r)}+\mathbf n_e^{(r)}.
 \label{eq:v2_waveform_observation}
\end{equation}
Here $\mathbf h_{e,q}$ is the PSF restricted to $\mathcal W_e$ and includes
the fractional delay, fractional Doppler, and template synchronization offset.
The remaining terms represent unresolved targets, clutter, unassociated
multipath, and receiver noise.  Their conditional null covariance is denoted by
$\mathbf R_{0,e}$.  A normalized template $\mathbf w_e$ produces
$z_e^{(r)}=\mathbf w_e^{H}\mathbf y_e^{(r)}$ and the waveform-equivalent SINR
\begin{equation}
 \gamma_e^{\rm wf}=
 \frac{\sigma_{\alpha,e}^{2}
       |\mathbf w_e^{H}\mathbf h_{e,q}|^2}
      {\mathbf w_e^{H}\mathbf R_{0,e}\mathbf w_e}.
 \label{eq:v2_waveform_sinr}
\end{equation}
The corresponding window-capture factor is
\begin{equation}
 \eta_e^{\rm wf}=
 \frac{|\mathbf w_e^{H}\mathbf h_{e,q}|^2}
      {\|\mathbf h_{e,q}\|_2^2}.
 \label{eq:v2_waveform_capture}
\end{equation}
The V1 sinc product $\eta_e^{\rm c}$ is retained as a coarse approximation to
\eqref{eq:v2_waveform_capture}; it is not treated as an independent physical
gain in the calibrated path.

\subsection{Finite-Look Local Statistic}

To avoid overloading the DD-grid delay dimension $L$, the number of
incoherently integrated frames is denoted by $K_{\rm look}$.  Define
\begin{equation}
 E_e=\sum_{r=1}^{K_{\rm look}}
 \frac{|z_e^{(r)}|^2}{\mathbf w_e^{H}\mathbf R_{0,e}\mathbf w_e}.
 \label{eq:v2_integrated_energy}
\end{equation}
Under the independent fast-fluctuation model,
$E_e\sim\operatorname{Gamma}(K_{\rm look},1)$ under $\mathcal H_0$ and
$E_e\sim\operatorname{Gamma}(K_{\rm look},1+\gamma_e^{\rm wf})$ under
$\mathcal H_1$.  The null-centred local log-likelihood statistic is
\begin{equation}
 \ell_e=
 \frac{\gamma_e^{\rm wf}}{1+\gamma_e^{\rm wf}}
 \left(E_e-K_{\rm look}\right).
 \label{eq:v2_waveform_llr}
\end{equation}
Its mean shift and null variance are
\begin{equation}
 \delta_e=\frac{K_{\rm look}(\gamma_e^{\rm wf})^2}
 {1+\gamma_e^{\rm wf}},\qquad
 v_{0,e}=\frac{K_{\rm look}(\gamma_e^{\rm wf})^2}
 {(1+\gamma_e^{\rm wf})^2}.
 \label{eq:v2_waveform_llr_moments}
\end{equation}

\subsection{Reporting Mixture and Calibrated Fusion Threshold}

Let $\chi_e$ be the finite-blocklength success probability of the report.  The
post-report statistic has hypothesis-dependent distribution
\begin{equation}
 F_{h,e}(x)=\chi_e F_{h,e}^{\rm succ}(x)
 +(1-\chi_e)F_e^{\rm fail}(x),\qquad h\in\{0,1\}.
 \label{eq:v2_report_mixture}
\end{equation}
For $S_q=\sum_{e\in\mathcal S_q}w_e\widetilde\ell_e$, let
$F_{h,q}^{\mathcal S}$ denote the resulting fused distribution.  The
calibrated threshold and predicted detection probability are
\begin{equation}
 \xi_q(\mathcal S)=
 \left(F_{0,q}^{\mathcal S}\right)^{-1}(1-P_{\rm FA}),\qquad
 \widehat P_{D,q}^{\rm cal}(\mathcal S)=
 1-F_{1,q}^{\mathcal S}\!\left(\xi_q(\mathcal S)\right).
 \label{eq:v2_calibrated_pd}
\end{equation}
For a singleton, the Gamma--Gaussian mixture is evaluated directly.  For
independent multi-report fusion, the current implementation estimates the null
quantile by deterministic Monte Carlo.  Correlated or non-erasure reporting
retains the V1 moment approximation and is outside the phase-1 claim.

\subsection{Phase-1 Interface to V1 Selection}

Phase 1 changes only the detector threshold after a V1-selected set has been
formed.  It therefore measures calibration error without confounding it with a
different selected set, report payload, or serial delay.  In a later phase,
$\widehat P_{D,q}^{\rm cal}$ may replace the V1 Gaussian approximation inside
the greedy marginal.  Until that coupled selector is implemented and validated,
no waveform-calibrated selection-gain claim is made.

\subsection{Scope}

This extension validates the mapping from a compact no-cyclic-prefix OTFS PSF
to finite-look detection statistics.  Pulse shaping, a tapped time-varying
channel, synchronization estimation, channel estimation, and waveform-induced
cross-report dependence remain outside the present model.  The V1 analytical
protocol and its released results remain the fallback reference.
