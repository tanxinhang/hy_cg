\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:introduction}

Integrated sensing and communication (ISAC) reuses spectrum and hardware
for communication and sensing~\cite{liu_dual_functional_6g,gonzalez_revolution_2024}.
UAV mobility extends coverage~\cite{mu_uav_isac,meng_uav_isac_challenges},
while cooperation supplies complementary target observations~\cite{meng_cooperative_isac_2025}.

Cooperation nevertheless creates a reporting problem. An observation follows
the bistatic sensing chain $i\!\rightarrow\!q\!\rightarrow\!j$, but its soft
statistic must subsequently reach a fusion UAV. Its value therefore depends on
both sensing quality and reporting reliability~\cite{yuan_dd_isac,
keskin_mimo_otfs_isac,chen_node_selection_backhaul_2025}. Existing node-selection
and backhaul-aware methods address parts of this coupling, while distributed
detection models often abstract the OTFS delay--Doppler (DD) geometry
~\cite{chawla_massive_mimo_dd,chawla_parameter_detection_mimo_wsn,
meng_network_level_isac_2024}.

Backhaul-aware selection optimizes reporting to a fusion center~\cite{chen_node_selection_backhaul_2025};
target-handover methods exchange track information across base stations
~\cite{ge_handover,bai_handover}. We instead study per-target fusion placement
before bistatic OTFS observation selection. The resulting local/remote
classification changes packet count, delivery, and detector-aligned C2F.
The contribution is this coupling and its controlled evaluation.

We study prior-assisted confirmation or re-detection and introduce target-local
fusion. For each target, the method chooses the UAV nearest its predicted
position, without accessing the current target truth. It then constructs the
target-specific reporting graph and applies a detector-aligned adaptive C2F
selector. This decomposition separates fusion placement from observation
selection. The outer distance rule is a reproducible heuristic rather than a
joint optimum.

Our contributions are threefold.
\begin{itemize}
	\item We introduce prediction-only target-local fusion for multi-UAV bistatic
	OTFS-ISAC. The target-specific fusion UAV converts its own observations into
	local evidence and exposes an explicit remote-reporting graph.

	\item We integrate this architecture with a detector-aligned adaptive C2F
	selector. The selector accounts for finite-blocklength delivery and reduces
	the number of waveform-level DD refinements relative to full refinement.

	\item We isolate the architectural effect through a 1000-trial paired comparison,
	a controlled fusion-rule ablation, and operating-boundary sweeps. The evidence
	supports communication savings with no statistically resolved detection
	difference from Sensing-SINR in the tested setting.
\end{itemize}
