
\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

This paper investigated communication-constrained cooperative sensing in a
multi-UAV OTFS-ISAC network. We developed a communication-error-calibrated and
C2F DD-aware Lagrangian link-selection method that jointly accounts for
marginal deflection gain, reporting reliability, exchange delay, and
DD-domain energy-capture loss. By calibrating exchanged soft statistics
according to reporting reliability, the proposed method reduces the impact of
unreliable reports on the fused statistic. The C2F DD-aware design further
applies local DD-grid refinement only to shortlisted candidates, thereby
recovering useful DD-domain energy while avoiding unnecessary fine-grid
evaluations.

Simulation results showed that the proposed method retains most of the
all-neighbor detection gain with much lower signaling overhead and exchange
delay. Under the same per-target selected-link budget, it achieves higher
detection probability than Sensing-SINR-based selection and provides detection
performance comparable to the Top-K Deflection baseline with lower delay.
Ablation and robustness results further support the benefits of
communication-error calibration, target-priority weighting, delay-aware
Lagrangian pricing, and C2F DD refinement.

Future work will extend the current geometry-driven soft-information
abstraction toward waveform-level OTFS-ISAC receiver modeling, more realistic
inter-UAV reporting protocols, and joint detection-tracking design for dynamic
multi-target UAV sensing networks.
