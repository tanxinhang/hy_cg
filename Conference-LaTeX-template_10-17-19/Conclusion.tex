\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Target-local fusion retains evidence at its producing UAV before adaptive C2F
selects remote reports. In 1000 paired trials at the nominal work point
($4000$~m, $50~\mathrm m^2$) it achieved $P_D=0.9764$ with
no statistically resolved detection difference from Sensing-SINR and 83.7\%
less payload and serial reporting delay. That reading is work-point specific:
at the low-RCS work point ($600$~m, $0.1~\mathrm m^2$) the same paired
comparison against Sensing-SINR is resolved and negative ($-0.0260$,
$[-0.0334,-0.0185]$), so there the method buys 51.9\% of the payload and delay
for $0.026$ detection probability. The contribution is architectural
communication efficiency under independent LLRs, fixed packets, and
prior-assisted sensing. Placement remains heuristic, detector prediction
approximate, and performance sensitive to belief error and sparse deployment.
