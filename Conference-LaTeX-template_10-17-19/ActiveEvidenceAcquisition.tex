\section{Active Evidence Acquisition}
\label{sec:active-evidence}

The proposed system is organized around a single question: under shared
sensing, communication, computation, and energy constraints, which evidence
should the UAV network actively acquire, transport, and fuse for detection?
This formulation replaces a version-by-version accumulation of modules with a
single progression from passive observation selection to active evidence
acquisition.

\subsection{Detector-consistent information}

For $L$ independent complex samples, let
\begin{equation}
z_\ell|H_0\sim\mathcal{CN}(0,\sigma^2),\qquad
z_\ell|H_1\sim\mathcal{CN}(0,(1+\gamma)\sigma^2).
\end{equation}
The forward and reverse KL divergences are
\begin{align}
D_{\rm KL}(P_1\Vert P_0)&=L[\gamma-\log(1+\gamma)],\\
D_{\rm KL}(P_0\Vert P_1)&=L\left[\log(1+\gamma)-
\frac{\gamma}{1+\gamma}\right].
\end{align}
Their sum,
\begin{equation}
J(P_1,P_0)=\frac{L\gamma^2}{1+\gamma},
\end{equation}
equals the centered exact-LLR mean gap. Hence, information is an optimization
layer derived from the implemented detector rather than an unrelated
surrogate. If an observed report-erasure variable is independent of the
hypothesis and has success probability $\chi$, the received divergence is
$\chi D_{\rm KL}$. Under conditional independence, received information is
additive across observations.

\subsection{Robust mixed-mode observations}

An active action is $a=(i,j,q,m,f_q)$, where UAV $i$ illuminates target $q$,
UAV $j$ receives the echo, $m$ selects sensing power, independent looks, and
delay--Doppler refinement, and $f_q$ is the fusion UAV. For aspect scenario
$s$, a bundle $B$ has
\begin{equation}
I_s(B)=\sum_{a\in B}I_{a,s},\qquad
I^{\rm rob}(B)=\min_{s\in\mathcal S}I_s(B).
\end{equation}
Because scenario-dependent target responses may reverse view rankings, the
candidate generator retains robust singletons, scenario-wise leaders, and
complementary pairs. This construction targets multi-view information
complementarity rather than uniform SNR diversity.

Active power also changes interference at other receivers, so the complete
model has $I_{a,s}=I_{a,s}(\mathbf{x})$. To preserve a linear global master,
the current implementation evaluates every active column under a conservative
full-load interference envelope. This exposes the power externality without
claiming equivalence to selected-load interference coupling.

\subsection{Integrated resource allocation}

A branch-and-bound oracle prices target--fusion link--mode bundles using an
optimistic scenario-information bound. Each resulting active column is then
evaluated with the mixed-mode exact LLR and true report erasures. The global
binary master selects one column per target and lexicographically minimizes
the worst detection deficit, total detection deficit, energy, remote reports,
and CPU cycles, subject to transmitter, receiver, fusion, reporting, and
computation budgets. KL or Jeffreys information guides candidate generation;
calibrated worst-scenario $P_D$ at the target $P_{FA}$ determines the
operational schedule.

The local oracle is exact over its declared candidate pool when untruncated,
and the global MILP is exact over the generated columns. These certificates do
not imply exactness over the complete fleet-wide active-action universe.

\subsection{Evidence and boundary}

Across 90 paired target instances from nine geometry clusters, active modes
increased robust received information by $4.6456$ on average (95\% cluster-
bootstrap CI: $[1.8370,8.8870]$) and worst-scenario $P_D$ by $0.03025$
($[0.01998,0.04006]$). Mean scenario $P_{FA}$ remained near $0.05$. The
physical-statistic and erasure streams now use common random numbers across
fusion destinations; the previous fusion-sensitive fleet experiment is
therefore superseded and requires a corrected multi-seed rerun. The current
evidence supports a pair-level average detection consequence in a finite-
scenario, single-interval setting. Sequential posterior updates, trajectory
control, correlated joint likelihoods, a fleet-level detection gain, and a
fleet-wide branch-price-and-cut certificate remain outside the present claim.
