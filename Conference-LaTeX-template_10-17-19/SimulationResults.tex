\subsection{Protocol and Nominal Configuration}

We evaluate prior-assisted confirmation in a $4000\,\mathrm{m}\times
4000\,\mathrm{m}$ region with $M=15$ UAVs and $Q=10$ targets. Table~
\ref{tab:simulation_parameters} lists the released V1 configuration. All main
methods use the same paired geometries, detector randomness, physical model,
and observation caps. The confirmatory comparison uses 1000
Monte-Carlo trials. Fusion and operating-envelope sweeps use 100 trials per
condition with seed 2026 and are therefore diagnostic rather than substitutes
for the main evidence.

\subsection{Confirmatory Main Comparison}

Figure~\ref{fig:v1_main} compares target-local adaptive C2F with two strong
rules under common caps. Exact-marginal greedy uses the deflection-based fair
utility without detector-aligned C2F replay; Sensing-SINR ranks nominal sensing
strength and matches V1's per-target observation counts. V1 attains
$P_D=0.9762$ (95\% CI: $[0.9730,0.9790]$), versus 0.9411 for exact-marginal
greedy and 0.9783 for Sensing-SINR. Worst-target values are 0.967, 0.928, and
0.970. The paired V1-minus-baseline intervals are $+0.0351$
($[0.0310,0.0392]$) and $-0.0021$ ($[-0.0051,0.0009]$), respectively.
Thus, V1 improves on exact-marginal selection but is statistically tied with
Sensing-SINR in detection probability.

The communication comparison is not tied. V1 selects 12.101 observations and
sends 0.751 remote reports on average, corresponding to 0.481~kbit and
0.801~ms. Sensing-SINR selects the same 12.101 observations but sends 4.595
reports, 2.941~kbit, and 4.901~ms. V1 therefore reduces payload and serial delay
by 83.7\% without a significant $P_D$ change. A separate MC=200 control applies
the same detector-PD greedy rule after refining all candidates. Adaptive C2F
uses 61.06 rather than 853.65 fine evaluations (92.85\% fewer) and attains
$P_D=0.980$ versus 0.973 for full refinement. The paired difference is 0.0070
($[0.0029,0.0111]$), consistent with shortlist regularization under belief
mismatch; full refinement is therefore a computational control, not an oracle.

\subsection{Why Target-Local Fusion Helps}

Figure~\ref{fig:fusion_ablation} changes only the fusion destination rule. The
target-agnostic max-in-rate rule gives $P_D=0.919$, worst-target $P_D=0.870$,
6.65 remote reports, and 7.093~ms. A max-min-rate alternative gives 0.969,
0.940, 6.40, and 6.827~ms. Nearest-target fusion reaches 0.982 and 0.950 with
only 0.74 remote reports and 0.789~ms. It also reduces selected observations
from 19.89 and 15.61 to 12.24. The mechanism is stronger than short-link
selection alone: a fusion UAV can retain every statistic it produces locally,
with unit reliability and no packet cost.

The reporting structure explains the difference. Max-in-rate and max-min-rate
assign all ten targets to one fusion UAV on average. Nearest-target assigns
them across 7.13 UAVs and lowers the mean conflict-graph requirement from
6.65/6.40 to 0.70 slots. Under nearest-target fusion, many trials need no
remote slot at all. V1 still reports measured serial latency; conflict slots
are retained only as a structural diagnostic.

\subsection{Operating Envelope and Design Choices}

Figure~\ref{fig:operating_envelope} summarizes six controlled scans. First,
V1 remains near $P_D=0.99$ for position-error standard deviations up to 50~m,
then decreases to 0.982 at 150~m, 0.937 at 300~m, and 0.900 at 500~m. Belief
capture remains 0.991 at 500~m; the degradation is therefore more consistent with
prediction-induced fusion and link mismatch than with DD-gate misses alone.
Second, changing $R_{\min}$ from 0.1 to 2~Mbit/s leaves $P_D=0.982$ and all
targets feasible because local observations bypass the reporting threshold.
This insensitivity is specific to the present target-local architecture.

Topology is the next boundary. Increasing the square side from 2500 to 5500~m
changes $P_D$ from 0.992 to 0.932 and reports from 0.05 to 2.67. Increasing
the fleet from 10 to 20 UAVs at $Q=10$ changes $P_D$ from 0.955 to 0.984 while
reducing reports from 2.13 to 0.22. These scans vary density implicitly and
must not be combined into an independent density effect.

The communication price exhibits a broad plateau. Setting $\lambda_c=0$
requires 4.96 reports at $P_D=0.983$, whereas $\lambda_c=0.005$ uses 0.74
reports at $P_D=0.982$. Values from 0.0025 to 0.02 have overlapping MC=100
uncertainty, so 0.005 is treated as a stable engineering point rather than a
statistically unique optimum. Finally, 2048 channel uses yields
$P_D=0.982$ and 0.789~ms. The 1024-use setting gives $P_D=0.981$ and
0.363~ms, while 3072 uses gives 0.981 and 1.216~ms. A paired high-MC
comparison is required before changing the blocklength.
