\section{Low-RCS Evidence Rescue}
\label{sec:low-rcs-rescue}

The proposed contribution is not cooperative illumination by itself. Existing
multi-static and cell-free ISAC studies already coordinate transmitters,
receivers, beamformers, groups, and power. We instead ask whether a constrained
multi-UAV network can create, preserve, and fuse sufficient
hypothesis-separating evidence to detect a target with smaller radar cross
section (RCS). This yields the end-to-end chain
\begin{equation}
\text{weak-echo deficit}\rightarrow\text{evidence formation}
\rightarrow\text{evidence transport}\rightarrow\text{likelihood fusion}
\rightarrow P_D@P_{FA}.
\end{equation}

\subsection{Minimum detectable RCS}

For feasible network decisions $\mathcal X$, required worst-target detection
probability $P_D^{\rm req}$, and false-alarm limit $\alpha$, we define
\begin{equation}
\sigma_{\rm det}^{\star}=\inf\left\{\sigma>0:\exists\mathbf{x}\in\mathcal X,
\min_qP_{D,q}(\sigma;\mathbf{x})\ge P_D^{\rm req},\quad
\max_qP_{FA,q}(\sigma;\mathbf{x})\le\alpha\right\}.
\end{equation}
A finite RCS sweep reports the conservative bracket
$\sigma_{\rm det}^{\star}\in(\sigma^-,\sigma^+]$, where $\sigma^-$ is the
largest tested failure below the first tested pass $\sigma^+$. No interpolation
is used when the empirical sweep is sparse or non-monotone.

\subsection{Formation, transport, and fusion}

An observation $a=(i,j,q,m)$ selects an illuminating UAV $i$, echo receiver
$j$, target $q$, and acquisition mode $m$. For scenario $s$, its local exact-LLR
information is $D_{a,s}$. For bundle $B_q$ and final fusion UAV $f_q$, define
\begin{align}
I_{q,s}^{\rm gen}(B_q)&=\sum_{a\in B_q}D_{a,s},\\
I_{q,s}^{\rm rx}(B_q,f_q)&=\sum_{a\in B_q}
\chi_{j(a)f_q}D_{a,s},\\
\mathcal L_{q,s}^{\rm net}&=I_{q,s}^{\rm gen}-I_{q,s}^{\rm rx},\\
\rho_q^{\rm net}&=\min_s\frac{I_{q,s}^{\rm rx}}
{I_{q,s}^{\rm gen}+\epsilon}.
\end{align}
Here $\chi_{j(a)f_q}$ is the observed, hypothesis-independent report-success
probability. A lossless-report diagnostic sets $\chi=1$ while preserving the
sensing bundle and modes. It therefore separates evidence-formation headroom
from transport headroom without changing the physical sensing decision.

Observation processing is charged to its actual receiver,
\begin{equation}
C_j^{\rm rx}=\sum_{a:j(a)=j}
\left[L_m(C_{\rm MF}+C_{\rm LLR})+
\mathbf 1_{r_m}C_{\rm DD}\right],
\end{equation}
whereas aggregation is charged to the final fusion UAV,
\begin{equation}
C_f^{\rm fus}=C_0+C_1|B_f|+C_3|B_f|^3.
\end{equation}
Every UAV obeys $C_u^{\rm rx}+C_u^{\rm fus}\le\bar C_u$.

\subsection{Integrated algorithm}

The local pricing oracle constructs aspect-robust link--mode bundles using KL
or Jeffreys information derived from the implemented exact LLR. Before costly
Monte Carlo column evaluation, an optional analytical fusion screen retains
the strongest destinations by robust received information and one
communication-locality anchor. The global binary master selects one column per
target and solves
\begin{equation}
\operatorname{lexmin}\left(d_{\max},\sum_qd_q,
\sum_q\max_s\mathcal L_{q,s}^{\rm net},E,N_{\rm remote},C\right),
\end{equation}
subject to transmitter, receiver, reporting, final-fusion, computation, and
energy limits. Detection fairness therefore remains primary, while evidence
loss breaks statistical ties before resource cost.

The analytical fusion screen is not a certificate over removed destinations.
The local solver may be exact over its declared candidate pool, and the master
is exact over generated columns; neither statement establishes full
action-space optimality. Receiver-local aggregation and multi-tier fusion are
deferred until a lossless-report oracle demonstrates material transport
headroom and an aggregate-packet reliability model is available.

To protect weak targets, the complete bundle returned by information pricing is
inserted as a rescue column even when exhaustive mode enumeration uses a
narrower link set. Pricing width is therefore decoupled from enumeration width.
We also distinguish the operational requirement $P_D^{\rm req}$ from a design
target $P_D^{\rm des}\ge P_D^{\rm req}$. Setting
$P_D^{\rm des}>P_D^{\rm req}$ preserves validation margin; setting
$P_D^{\rm des}=1$ makes the first lexicographic stage a pure max--min detector
design instead of stopping as soon as every design estimate reaches the
operational threshold.

\subsection{Current evidence and claim boundary}

In one corrected 15-UAV/10-target instance, active acquisition achieved
mean/worst target $P_D=0.78951/0.20776$, compared with
$0.77596/0.20776$ for fixed nominal acquisition, while mean $P_{FA}$ remained
near $0.05$. Physical-statistic random streams were common across fusion
destinations, so routing no longer resamples the echo. The result validates the
execution path and a mean improvement in that instance; it does not demonstrate
a fleet-population effect, a worst-target advantage, or a reduced minimum
detectable RCS. Those claims require paired multi-seed RCS sweeps and the
formation-versus-transport headroom ablation.

A subsequent three-geometry screen at an explicit 17.5-dB radar net-gain
coordinate and $P_D^{\rm des}=1$ reached mean worst-target
$P_D=0.9857$ and mean-target $P_D=0.9981$. The result demonstrates a feasible
remediation point, but not yet a promotion claim: the hardware coordinate must
be mapped to a real platform and the maximum scenario false-alarm rate requires
higher-resolution simultaneous calibration.
