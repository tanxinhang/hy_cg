###### Strong aspects: Comments to the author: what are the strong aspects of the paper

The paper addresses communication-constrained cooperative sensing in multi-UAV OTFS-ISAC systems. It jointly considers sensing contribution, reporting reliability, communication delay, and delay-Doppler energy-capture efficiency in the link-selection process. The coarse-to-fine strategy also reduces unnecessary fine-grid evaluations. The paper is generally well organized and includes comparative, sensitivity, ablation, and robustness studies.

###### Weak aspects: Comments to the author: what are the weak aspects of the paper?

1. A clearer explanation is needed regarding the relationship between the bistatic sensing link and the soft-information reporting link, including the source, destination, and fusion node of the reported information.
2. Regarding the DD-domain modeling, both the gain formulation and the C2F refinement appear largely heuristic. In particular, the computation of (\eta^{\mathrm{loc}}_{ijq}) and the rationale behind the associated parameters should be provided.
3. More rigorous probabilistic justification is required for the communication-reliability function and for the proposed calibration of the soft-information mean and variance.
4. In its current form, the optimization framework and algorithm description are not sufficiently complete. The paper would benefit from a formal problem formulation, detailed pseudocode, clearer treatment of the budget constraints, and further discussion of the optimality of the greedy procedure and the overall computational complexity.
5. Experimental validation remains limited by the use of an abstract system model. Several parameters and error models require clearer definitions. In addition, confidence intervals, runtime comparisons, or waveform-level OTFS simulations would further strengthen the validation.

###### Recommended changes: Please indicate any changes that should be made to the paper if accepted.

Please refer to the weak aspects above.

###### Strong aspects: Comments to the author: what are the strong aspects of the paper

The paper addresses a relevant problem of communication-constrained cooperative sensing in multi-UAV OTFS-ISAC networks. The proposed framework jointly considers sensing contribution, reporting reliability, exchange delay, and DD-domain energy efficiency. The triplet-level formulation and the coarse-to-fine link-selection strategy are well motivated. The simulation study is relatively comprehensive, including baseline comparisons, sensitivity analysis, ablation studies, and robustness evaluations.

###### Weak aspects: Comments to the author: what are the weak aspects of the paper?

Several key components of the proposed framework rely on simplified or heuristic models whose physical interpretation is not sufficiently explained. In particular, the definitions of the DD-domain refinement gain and communication-reliability calibration require further justification. Some baseline schemes and the calculation of signaling overhead and exchange delay are also not described clearly enough for reproducibility.

###### Recommended changes: Please indicate any changes that should be made to the paper if accepted.

1. Please provide a clearer definition of the locally recoverable DD-domain energy used in the C2F refinement. A mathematical expression or a brief computational procedure would improve reproducibility.
2. The physical interpretation and justification of the reporting-reliability model should be further clarified, especially because it directly affects the calibrated soft-information statistics.
3. Please clarify the reporting architecture and the relationship between the bistatic sensing pair and the soft-information reporting link, since the same ordered pair is used for both roles.
4. Several baseline schemes used in the simulations should be defined more explicitly. Please briefly describe their selection rules and clarify whether the comparisons are performed under equivalent resource constraints.
5. Please explain more clearly how the overall signaling overhead and exchange delay are calculated from the individual selected links, particularly whether links are assumed to report sequentially or in parallel.

###### Strong aspects: Comments to the author: what are the strong aspects of the paper

The proposed link-selection framework provides a reasonable trade-off between cooperative detection performance and communication cost, reducing signaling overhead and reporting delay while retaining most of the all-neighbor detection gain.

###### Weak aspects: Comments to the author: what are the weak aspects of the paper?

1. The proposed target-dependent scheduling requires the target position, velocity, and RCS to calculate the delay, Doppler, DD-bin index, and sensing gain. Since the paper focuses on target detection, it is unclear how this information is obtained before detection. The authors should clarify the source of this prior information and evaluate the impact of target-state uncertainty.
2. For triplet (i,j,q), UAV i transmits the sensing signal and UAV j receives the echo, implying that the soft statistic is generated at UAV j. However, the reporting rate, reliability, and delay are defined for the link from i to j, while the fusion node is not specified. The authors should clarify the reporting direction and the overall fusion architecture.
3. The communication reliability is modeled as \chi_{ij}=\gamma_{ij}^{c}/(\gamma_{ij}^{c}+\gamma_{req}) in equation (4), the authors should justify this reliability function.
