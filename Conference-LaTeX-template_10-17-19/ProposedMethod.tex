
\section{Proposed C2F DD-Aware Lagrangian Link Selection}
\label{sec:proposed_algorithm}

We select a compact set of cooperative candidates whose marginal fusion gain
justifies their reporting cost. The selection is implemented as a low-complexity
greedy rule guided by a Lagrangian marginal-gain score. For target $q$, the
feasible candidate set is
\begin{equation}
	\mathcal{E}_q =
	\left\{
	(i,j)\in\mathcal{E}:
	R_{ij}\geq R_{\min},
	(l_{ijq},k_{ijq})~\text{is DD-valid}
	\right\}.
	\label{eq:feasible_candidate_set}
\end{equation}
Here $c_{ijq}$ and the DD-valid condition are defined in
Section~\ref{sec:system_model}. We use
$\tilde c_{ijq}=10^3c_{ijq}$ as the delay in milliseconds.

Given the current selected set $\mathcal S_q$, the target-priority coefficient
is updated as
\begin{equation}
	\alpha_q =
	\frac{\partial P_{D,q}}{\partial D_q}
	+
	\mu_{\alpha}
	\frac{[D_{\min}-D_q(\mathcal S_q)]^+}{D_{\min}},
	\label{eq:target_priority_compact}
\end{equation}
where $[x]^+=\max(x,0)$. The derivative follows from
\eqref{eq:pd_approx} with a small regularization when $D_q$ is close to zero,
and $\alpha_q^{(0)}$ denotes the value evaluated at
$\mathcal S_q=\emptyset$.

For candidate $(i,j)$ and target $q$, the marginal calibrated deflection gain is
\begin{equation}
	\Delta D_{ijq}
	=
	D_q\!\left(\mathcal S_q\cup\{(i,j)\}\right)
	-
	D_q(\mathcal S_q),
	\label{eq:marginal_deflection_gain}
\end{equation}
where $D_q(\cdot)$ uses the calibrated statistics in
\eqref{eq:comm_error_calibration}. The Lagrangian score is
\begin{equation}
	\Delta\mathcal L_{ijq}
	=
	\alpha_q\Delta D_{ijq}
	-
	\lambda_c\tilde c_{ijq},
	\label{eq:lagrangian_score_compact}
\end{equation}
where $\lambda_c>0$ controls the delay penalty.

To reduce local DD-grid refinement cost, we use a C2F strategy. Let
$L_{\mathrm{all}}$ be the number of feasible candidates before screening and
$L_{\mathrm{short}}\ll L_{\mathrm{all}}$ be the shortlist size. In the coarse
stage, candidates are ranked by
\begin{equation}
	\Delta\mathcal L_{ijq}^{\mathrm c}
	=
	\alpha_q^{(0)}
	\Delta D_{ijq}^{\mathrm c}
	-
	\lambda_c\tilde c_{ijq},
	\label{eq:coarse_lagrangian_score_compact}
\end{equation}
where $\Delta D_{ijq}^{\mathrm c}$ is computed using the coarse DD gain in
\eqref{eq:coarse_dd_gain}. Only the top $L_{\mathrm{short}}$ candidates are
retained for fine refinement.

The fine stage applies \eqref{eq:fine_dd_gain} only to the shortlisted
candidates and recomputes \eqref{eq:marginal_deflection_gain} and
\eqref{eq:lagrangian_score_compact}. Starting from
$\mathcal S_q=\emptyset$, the algorithm repeatedly selects the remaining
candidate with the largest positive $\Delta\mathcal L_{ijq}$ and updates
$D_q(\mathcal S_q)$ and $\alpha_q$. It stops when no positive-score candidate
exists, the link-number limit is reached, or all active targets with feasible
candidates satisfy $D_q(\mathcal S_q)\geq D_{\min}$. C2F reduces fine DD-grid
evaluations from $\mathcal O(QL_{\mathrm{all}}(2W+1)^2)$ to
$\mathcal O(QL_{\mathrm{short}}(2W+1)^2)$.
